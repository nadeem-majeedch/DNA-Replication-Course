Learning Outcomes



Upon completing all laboratory exercises, students will be able to:
  • Model DNA replication structures and processes

  • Quantify and assess DNA quality

  • Perform agarose gel electrophoresis

  • Conduct and analyze PCR experiments

  • Interpret replication-related data

  • Apply computational approaches to replication analysis




    • Laboratory Rules



  • Arrive on time — pre-lab preparation is required

  • Wear PPE — lab coat, gloves, safety glasses at all times in wet lab

  • No food or drink in the laboratory

  • Clean up your workspace after each experiment

  • Report all accidents immediately to the instructor

  • Follow SOPs — Standard Operating Procedures must be followed for all procedures

  • Dispose of waste properly — follow institutional waste disposal guidelines

  • No unauthorized experiments — only perform approved procedures

  • Record everything — keep detailed notes in this workbook

  • Academic integrity — your work must be your own




    • Safety Summary



    Chemical Hazards


    ChemicalHazardPPE Required

    Ethidium bromideMutagenDouble gloves, lab coat

    Biological Hazards


  • DNA samples: Follow institutional biosafety guidelines

  • PCR products: Handle with care to prevent contamination


    • Equipment Hazards


  • Electrophoresis: Electrical hazard — never touch running equipment

  • Microwave: Burn hazard — handle hot flasks with care

  • UV transilluminator: Eye hazard — use protective goggles


    • Emergency Procedures


  • Spill: Notify instructor, clean up per SOP

  • EtBr exposure: Wash thoroughly, notify instructor

  • Electrical shock: Disconnect power, seek medical attention

  • Eye exposure: Flush with water for 15 minutes, seek medical attention
