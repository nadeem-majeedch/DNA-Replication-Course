### Course Description

This comprehensive teaching package covers the complete molecular biology of DNA replication in both prokaryotic and eukaryotic systems. Designed for BS/MS students in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, Microbiology, Genetics, and related disciplines, the material progresses from foundational concepts to advanced research-level topics.

The package includes lecture notes, laboratory exercises, computational bioinformatics activities, assessment materials, and a complete presentation for classroom teaching.

---

### Prerequisites

- Basic cell biology (cell structure, cell division)
- Introductory genetics (chromosomes, genes, DNA structure)
- General biochemistry (nucleotides, phosphodiester bonds, protein structure)
- Familiarity with the central dogma of molecular biology

---

### Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:

1. Explain the molecular basis of semiconservative DNA replication
2. Describe and compare replication mechanisms in prokaryotes (E. coli) and eukaryotes (human cells)
3. Identify and explain the functions of all major replication proteins
4. Describe replication initiation, elongation, and termination in detail
5. Explain replication fidelity, proofreading, and repair mechanisms
6. Describe the end-replication problem and telomere biology
7. Explain replication stress, checkpoint mechanisms, and DNA damage responses
8. Interpret experimental data related to DNA replication
9. Use bioinformatics tools to analyze replication-related sequences and proteins
10. Connect replication mechanisms to disease, drug development, and biotechnology
