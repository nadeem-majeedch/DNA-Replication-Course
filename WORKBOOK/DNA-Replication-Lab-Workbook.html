Learning Outcomes

Upon completing all laboratory exercises, students will be able to:

  1. Model DNA replication structures and processes
  2. Quantify and assess DNA quality
  3. Perform agarose gel electrophoresis
  4. Conduct and analyze PCR experiments
  5. Interpret replication-related data
  6. Apply computational approaches to replication analysis

Laboratory Rules

  1. Arrive on time — pre-lab preparation is required
  2. Wear PPE — lab coat, gloves, safety glasses at all times in wet lab
  3. No food or drink in the laboratory
  4. Clean up your workspace after each experiment
  5. Report all accidents immediately to the instructor
  6. Follow SOPs — Standard Operating Procedures must be followed for all procedures
  7. Dispose of waste properly — follow institutional waste disposal guidelines
  8. No unauthorized experiments — only perform approved procedures
  9. Record everything — keep detailed notes in this workbook
  10. Academic integrity — your work must be your own

Safety Summary

Chemical Hazards

Chemical Hazard PPE Required
Ethidium bromide Mutagen Double gloves, lab coat
Agarose (hot) Burn hazard Heat-resistant gloves
TAE buffer Low hazard Gloves
Loading dye Low hazard Gloves

Biological Hazards

  • DNA samples: Follow institutional biosafety guidelines
  • PCR products: Handle with care to prevent contamination

Equipment Hazards

  • Electrophoresis: Electrical hazard — never touch running equipment
  • Microwave: Burn hazard — handle hot flasks with care
  • UV transilluminator: Eye hazard — use protective goggles

Emergency Procedures

  • Spill: Notify instructor, clean up per SOP
  • EtBr exposure: Wash thoroughly, notify instructor
  • Electrical shock: Disconnect power, seek medical attention
  • Eye exposure: Flush with water for 15 minutes, seek medical attention
