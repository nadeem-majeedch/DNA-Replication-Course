# DNA Replication Course — References

## Primary Textbooks

1. **Alberts B, Johnson A, Lewis J, et al.** *Molecular Biology of the Cell*, 7th Edition. W.W. Norton & Company, 2022.
   - Chapters 5 (DNA Replication), 17 (Cell-Cycle Control), 20 (DNA Repair)

2. **Lodish H, Berk A, Kaiser CA, et al.** *Molecular Cell Biology*, 9th Edition. W.H. Freeman, 2021.
   - Chapter 5 (DNA Replication, Repair, and Recombination)

3. **Watson JD, Baker TA, Bell SP, et al.** *Molecular Biology of the Gene*, 7th Edition. Pearson, 2014.
   - Part III (Genomes)

4. **Lewin B, Krebs JE, Kilpatrick ST, Goldstein ES.** *Lewin's GENES XII*, 12th Edition. Jones & Bartlett Learning, 2018.
   - Chapter 11 (DNA Replication)

5. **Nelson DL, Cox MM.** *Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry*, 8th Edition. W.H. Freeman, 2021.
   - Chapter 25 (DNA Metabolism)

6. **Kornberg A, Baker TA.** *DNA Replication*, 2nd Edition. W.H. Freeman, 1992.
   - Classic reference on prokaryotic replication

7. **Bell SP, Dutta A.** "DNA replication in the eukaryotic cell." *Annual Review of Cell Biology*, 2002, 18:305-339.
   - Comprehensive review of eukaryotic replication mechanisms

## Seminal Research Papers

8. **Meselson M, Stahl FW.** "The replication of DNA in Escherichia coli." *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 1958, 44(7):671-682.
   - The "most beautiful experiment in biology" proving semiconservative replication

9. **Okazaki R, Okazaki T, Sakabe K, et al.** "Mechanism of DNA chain growth. I. Possible discontinuity and unusual secondary structure of newly synthesized chains." *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 1968, 59(2):598-605.
   - Discovery of Okazaki fragments

10. **Greider CW, Blackburn EH.** "Identification of a specific telomere terminal transferase activity in Tetrahymena extracts." *Cell*, 1985, 43(2 Pt 1):405-413.
    - Discovery of telomerase activity

11. **Nakamura TM, Morin GB, Chapman KB, et al.** "Telomerase catalytic subunit homologs from fission yeast and human." *Science*, 1997, 277(5328):955-959.
    - Identification of TERT

12. **Mickel S, Bautz E, Bautz W.** "Isolation and characterization of an autonomously replicating sequence from Drosophila melanogaster." *Molecular and General Genetics*, 1982, 186:339-346.

13. **Stillman B.** "Origin recognition and the chromosome cycle." *FEBS Letters*, 2005, 579(4):877-884.
    - Review of ORC and origin recognition

14. **Bleichert F, Botchan MR.** "Mechanisms for initiating cellular DNA replication." *Science*, 2016, 351(6272):aad6104.
    - Comprehensive review of replication initiation

15. **Deegan TD, Diffley JFX.** "CMG: the ring that unlocks the road to replication." *Biochemical Society Transactions*, 2016, 44(1):50-57.
    - Review of CMG helicase mechanism

16. **Branzei D, Foiani M.** "Maintaining genome stability at the replication fork." *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology*, 2010, 11(3):208-220.
    - Comprehensive review of fork stability

17. **Lopes M, Cotta-Ramusino C, Pellicioli A, et al.** "The DNA replication checkpoint response stabilizes stalled replication forks." *Nature*, 2001, 412(6846):557-561.
    - Fork stabilization by checkpoints

18. **Cotta-Ramusino C, Fachinetti D, Bachi J, et al.** "Exo1 processes stalled replication forks and counteracts recombination in the replication checkpoint response." *Molecular Cell*, 2005, 17(1):153-159.

19. **Niida H, Nakanishi M.** "DNA damage checkpoints in mammals." *Mutagenesis*, 2006, 21(1):3-9.

20. **Halazonetis TD, Gorgoulis VG, Bartek J.** "An oncogene-induced DNA damage model for cancer development." *Science*, 2008, 319(5868):1352-1355.
    - Oncogene-induced replication stress
